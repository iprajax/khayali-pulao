## Full description (≤ 4000)
```
Khayali pulao (ख़याली पुलाव) is Hindi for daydreaming — literally "imaginary pulao", rice you cook only in your head. Every investor does it. Khayali Pulao does the maths properly.

A small bubble floats over your broker app (works with the Zerodha Kite app). Switch it on, tap a holding or a watchlist stock, and a sheet shows:

• MOVE % — What if it moves +0.5%, +5%, +15% or doubles? A ladder in 0.5% steps up to 5%, then 7, 10, 12, 15, 20 and beyond. Slider, step buttons and "target price → %". From your average price or today's price.
• VS FD — Did you actually beat a bank fixed deposit? Your real yearly return and inflation-adjusted gain, compared with savings, FD, PPF, gold, Nifty 50 and inflation. Every rate is yours to set.
• SPEND IT — Your profit in hand after estimated LTCG / STCG, STT and charges, and what it means: FD interest per month, months of expenses covered, 4%-rule income.
• GOAL — "I want ₹50 lakh." The rise and price needed, and roughly how many years at 8–25% a year.
• YEARS — Compounding over 1 to 30 years.
• REGRET — "Agar tab liya hota": what if you had bought cheaper?
• WHOLE PORTFOLIO — Daydream your total invested / current value at once.
• PULAO MATHS — Gains in cutting chais, Bhrigu Express tickets, a bhains for the village or a plot in Ballia — or your own dream.
• TONE — Serious (numbers only), Light, or Masala.

No broker app? Tap "Try the demo" for a fictional portfolio.

PRIVACY — NOTHING LEAVES YOUR PHONE
• Internet is used only to download the optional on-device AI model once from Hugging Face. Nothing is ever uploaded.
• The AI chat (Gemma 4) runs entirely on your phone.
• It uses Android's Accessibility Service only to read the prices and quantities your broker app already shows on screen, only while that app is open, and only to calculate projections on your phone.
• It never taps Buy or Sell and never places orders.
• No account, no login, no analytics, no ads. Nothing is collected or shared.
• Open source: github.com/iprajax/khayali-pulao

DISCLAIMER
Khayali Pulao is a calculator for daydreaming, not investment, tax or financial advice. Projections are hypothetical; tax and charges are estimates. Not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Zerodha. "Zerodha" and "Kite" are trademarks of their respective owner, used only to describe compatibility.
```
